# FAQ - Hotel Aurora (fixture data)

**Do you have a minimum group size?** Group rates start at 6 rooms; a single
room enquiry is welcome but goes through our regular booking page, not the
sales desk.

**Can you hold space provisionally?** Yes, a provisional hold is free for 14
days on request.

**Do you offer a discount for a large block?** Up to 15% off rack without
sign-off; anything deeper needs a manager's approval.

**What is included in a day delegate rate?** Room hire, AV, and two coffee
breaks. Lunch and dinner are quoted separately.

**Do you host weddings?** Yes, April to October, with a dedicated coordinator
for bookings above 15 rooms.
